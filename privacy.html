Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Overview

FunHub is a free website offering small games, generators and calculators. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the website and how it is used.

2. Information you enter into FunHub

FunHub's games, generators and calculators are designed to work in your browser. Information you enter into these tools is processed on your device and is not intentionally sent to or stored by FunHub's own servers.

For example, dates entered into the age calculator and text entered into the nickname generator are used to produce the requested result in your browser.

3. Google Analytics

FunHub may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are visited, how visitors arrive at the site, and how people interact with the site.

When Google Analytics is enabled, Google may collect information such as browser/device information, approximate location, referral information, page views and interactions. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies for measurement.

FunHub does not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, generated text, calculator values, or other directly identifying information to Google Analytics. FunHub may send limited event information such as the type of game started, a tool action, or a generator category so we can understand which features are useful.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

FunHub itself does not require an account or login. If Google Analytics is enabled, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure website usage. Your browser may provide controls for managing cookies.

5. Third-party services

FunHub is hosted using GitHub Pages. If Google Analytics is enabled, analytics data is processed by Google. These services have their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Children's privacy

FunHub is a general-audience website and does not intentionally ask visitors to provide personal information. We do not knowingly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords through the site's games, generators or calculators.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FunHub's features or services change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

8. Contact

If you have a privacy question about FunHub, please use the contact method provided with the FunHub project or its hosting repository.

9. More information

For information about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's published privacy information.
